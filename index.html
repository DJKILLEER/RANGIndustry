Applications

Where CRGO is Used

Cold Rolled Grain Oriented (CRGO) Electrical Steel is undoubtedly the most important soft magnetic material in use today. Whether in small transformers, distribution transformers, or large power transformers and generators — CRGO grain-oriented electrical steel is essential for energy-efficient electrical machines. Its low core loss and high permeability enable more economical and efficient transformers across all voltage classes.

Manufacturing Process

How We Build
Excellence

A rigorous 10-step manufacturing process that ensures every piece of CRGO lamination and core meets the highest standards of quality and precision.

Step-by-Step Process

Manufacturing Flow

01
Raw Material Receipt
CRGO material received from imports, suppliers, traders, or directly from manufacturing mills. Full coil form, verified on arrival.
02
Physical Inspection
Thorough physical inspection of grades and dimensions of all incoming material to verify compliance with order specifications.
03
Sorting & Grading
Material sorted and graded according to production requirements — M3, M4, M5, M6, HI-B, and other grades segregated.
04
Core Loss Testing
Testing of core losses and magnetic properties using digital iron loss tester before material enters the production line.
05
Precision Cutting
Cutting as per engineering drawings on calibrated shearing machines. Dimensions controlled to micron-level tolerances.
06
V-Notching & Holing
V-notching and holing performed in power press using specialized die tools as per customer specifications.
07
Continuous Monitoring
Continuous monitoring and supervision of quality, grade thickness, and dimensions throughout the production process.
08
Burr-Free Edges
Ensuring completely burr-free edges in all cutting, holing, and notching operations. De-burring machine deployed for final finishing.
09
Bundling & Weight Check
Careful bundling and weight verification of the finished product using calibrated scales before packing for transport.
10
Dispatch & Documentation
Dispatch with Challan copy and Invoice copy to the transporter. Complete traceability and documentation at every step.
🏗️
Building Factor (BF) Control

Building Factor (BF) is critical in determining No-Load Losses of a transformer, ranging from 1.08 to 1.35 for three-phase, three-limb cores. We actively reduce BF through proper material handling, maintaining appropriate process parameters, and controlling burr and edge camber levels throughout manufacturing.
